# IRIS 0.2.0 implementation and scientific audit

Audit date: **2026-09-06**

The final execution evidence, including the later compatibility matrix, security
audit, release build, and prioritized residual work, is recorded in
[`PROJECT_EXECUTION_REPORT.md`](PROJECT_EXECUTION_REPORT.md). The manuscript keeps
its earlier 212-test/54-subtest audit freeze as a historical statement; the record
below describes the final engineering tree.

## Executive assessment

IRIS has been reorganized from a collection of coupled astronomy scripts into a
coherent Python research platform. The current release has a strict data model,
reproducible local pipeline, conservative catalogue validation, immutable review
provenance, leakage-aware baselines, and a shadow-only irregular-time TS-JEPA
path.

At audit close, no known P0 or P1 defect remained inside the audited local release
boundary. That statement is deliberately narrower than “scientifically
validated” or “production ready.” The code now makes strong local integrity and
failure-handling guarantees, but it has not yet passed a prospective discovery
campaign, a live external-service qualification campaign, or a hardened
multi-user deployment review.

The governing safety boundary is:

> Models rank. Bound evidence and independent people decide.

IRIS does not expose an unattended TNS submission path. A score is not a
probability, a network failure is not a catalogue clearance, and a local hash is
an integrity identifier rather than authentication against a malicious system
administrator.

## What is genuinely strong

### Architecture and organization

- Maintained code lives under `src/iris/` with explicit modules for ingestion,
  feature extraction, scoring, validation, review, reporting policy, ledgers,
  manifests, and machine learning.
- `pyproject.toml` is the package and dependency authority. Optional astronomy,
  visualization, and ML dependencies are separated from the minimal runtime.
- TOML configuration is typed, rejects unknown keys and unsupported schema
  versions, validates broker selection, and resolves storage paths predictably.
- Root-era scripts are documented as legacy. The unsafe report-draft generator is
  a fail-closed tombstone in both historical locations; its implementation is
  retained only as non-executable Markdown for provenance.

### Measurement and ranking science

- `iris.photometry.v4` preserves magnitude, limit, and supplied
  forced/difference-flux semantics instead of silently mixing them.
- Photometric statistics are computed per survey/passband channel, preventing
  same-named filters from unrelated facilities from fabricating variability.
- Detection, uncertainty, quality, non-detection, missingness, and channel
  coverage remain visible. Missing errors are never silently replaced with zero.
- The heuristic is bounded, decomposed, versioned, configuration-driven, and
  labelled as a priority score—not a calibrated discovery probability.
- Queue construction is capacity-aware and deterministic, with separate anomaly
  and seeded random-audit routes. Audit entries carry their replay seed,
  population, inclusion probability, and propensity.

### Fail-closed evidence and review

- TNS, SkyBoT, SIMBAD, and VSX outcomes distinguish clear, match, error,
  disabled, stale, and pending states.
- Clear evidence requires a successful attempt, response digest, valid TTL, and
  binding to candidate identity, position, radius, service contract, and relevant
  epochs. TNS clearance binds both internal-name and cone searches. SkyBoT must
  clear every distinct usable detection epoch.
- Imported verification requires the exact versioned schema and complete
  provenance. Missing timestamps and attempt counts are not synthesized.
- SIMBAD context labels and their interpretation are preserved and hashed through
  analysis, candidate versions, dossiers, the ledger, and reporting preflight.
  A manual-review flag cannot be detached from the counterpart context that
  caused it.
- Catalogue clients receive the configured timeout and user-agent policy at the
  supported astroquery/client/session layers. Timeout behavior still requires
  recorded and live operational qualification.
- Candidate evidence records are self-verifying. Review-set assembly recomputes
  observation, candidate-version, preflight, and whole-record digests and rejects
  altered or mixed ranking/evidence artifacts.
- Reporting preflight accepts only the canonical versioned pipeline contract,
  verifies completed run publication, rebinds checks, recomputes policy digests,
  rechecks freshness, and requires exact-version human decisions.
- Ledger schema v4 archives first-seen candidate-version payloads. Reviews,
  catalogue adjudications, and outcomes are version-bound, and database triggers
  prevent scientific event rows from being updated or deleted.

### Reproducibility and failure recovery

- Scientific fingerprints bind normalized observations, relevant configuration,
  evidence, context, and policy while excluding volatile latency/timestamp noise
  from incomplete checks.
- Input bytes are read once, hashed, analyzed, and archived as the same snapshot.
  Round-trip-safe float serialization avoids near-equal-value collisions.
- Broker fetch publishes a content-bound CSV/sidecar pair through staged,
  same-filesystem no-clobber operations. A marked broker CSV with a missing or
  inconsistent sidecar is rejected by ingestion.
- Source manifests hash maintained source/config files rather than interpreter
  caches. Installed wheels hash the installed IRIS package and record the
  distribution version.
- A completed run manifest is durably written before candidate versions become
  reviewable in the ledger. A terminal-manifest failure leaves no authoritative
  candidate publication and records failure where possible.

### Machine-learning discipline

- The supervised baseline uses chronological train/calibration/test blocks,
  requires entity purging or an explicit unique-entity assertion, fits transforms
  on training data only, and separates ranking metrics from calibrated metrics.
- Average precision and cutoff metrics have explicit tie policies and are
  invariant to arbitrary input ordering.
- Anomaly scoring abstains when reference support is insufficient. Similarity
  indexes bind encoder, token-contract, and dataset-snapshot provenance and use
  deterministic terminal tie-breaking.
- TS-JEPA uses irregular-time tokens, contiguous masking, an EMA target encoder,
  context-only normalization, a missing-error sentinel, explicit flux detection
  flags, deterministic algorithms by default, repeated-mask evaluation, and
  collapse diagnostics.
- The `iris.light_curve_tokens.v3` contract is hashed and checked within batches,
  across train/validation splits, in checkpoints, during evaluation, and during
  embedding extraction. Checkpoint loading uses PyTorch's restricted
  `weights_only=True` path.
- JEPA is explicitly a shadow research representation learner. It does not bypass
  evidence gates and is not represented as a discovery classifier.

## What was going wrong, and how it was fixed

The audit used code inspection, small adversarial reproductions, fault injection,
focused tests, full-suite runs, installed-wheel tests, and offline workflow
smokes. Major corrected failure classes included:

| Area | Prior failure mode | Implemented correction |
|---|---|---|
| Ingestion | Ambiguous aliases, missing detection semantics, inverted/unknown quality flags, mutable-source rereads, duplicate observation IDs | Strict canonicalization, fail-closed semantics, byte snapshots, content-bound broker metadata, duplicate/revision checks |
| Photometry | Cross-survey pooling, zero-filled missing errors, survey-agnostic numeric filters, lossy float identity | Survey/passband channels, explicit missingness, survey-aware aliases, round-trip-safe serialization |
| Validation | Replayed clears, absent TTL/attempt/digest, incomplete TNS or SkyBoT coverage, stale epoch laundering, equal-time order dependence | Central binding contract, strict completed-check invariants, two-stage TNS, all-epoch SkyBoT, conservative conflict/freshness policy |
| Catalogue context | SIMBAD host/unknown labels were discarded while a mutable boolean survived | Canonical context schema; labels and interpretation are recomputed and bound end to end |
| Human review | Stale forms, self-asserted identity variants, policy downgrade, overwritten historical payloads | Exact-version writes, canonical principals, bound reviewer policy, append-only version/event history, stale-form rejection |
| Artifact review | Ranking rows and candidate JSON could be mixed or edited while retaining asserted version strings | Independent candidate-record and cross-artifact digest recomputation during review assembly |
| Run publication | Ledger candidates could exist after terminal-manifest failure | Manifest-first authoritative publication and compensating failed-state behavior |
| Reproducibility | Cache-sensitive source digests, path/mtime-sensitive science IDs, insertion-order ties, wall-clock freshness drift | Explicit source allowlist, content-derived IDs, stable tie policies, one captured evaluation instant |
| Baseline/JEPA | Optional entity leakage controls, mixed token contracts, unsafe checkpoint fallback, single-mask uncertainty | Required split policy, contract equality, restricted loading, repeated masks and confidence summaries |
| Legacy safety | Executable report-draft builders bypassed canonical preflight | Both legacy entry points replaced with fail-closed tombstones |

## What remains unproven or incomplete

These are not hidden defects. They are the evidence and operational work required
before stronger claims are justified.

1. **Prospective scientific value.** IRIS has not yet shown, on a locked future
   campaign, that it improves discoveries per fixed review/follow-up budget over
   the heuristic, supervised baseline, and prior workflow.
2. **Live-service qualification.** Catalogue adapters need recorded response
   fixtures, schema-change monitors, rate-limit tests, outage drills, and regular
   live canaries. Configured library/session timeouts reduce risk but are not yet
   proof of a hard end-to-end deadline for every third-party stack.
3. **Image-level evidence.** The platform consumes supplied forced/difference
   flux. It does not yet acquire, calibrate, or independently verify survey images,
   reference/difference stamps, or forced-photometry products.
4. **Identity and deployment security.** The loopback review UI has CSRF
   protection but no authentication, role provider, signed artifacts, secrets
   manager, production authorization policy, or hardened multi-user deployment.
5. **Training corpus and labels.** A versioned outcome taxonomy exists, but a
   curated, audited, sufficiently large label set and prospective prevalence
   estimates do not. The heuristic and JEPA embeddings must not be called
   calibrated probabilities.
6. **Population and calibration validity.** Host association, cross-survey
   calibration, anomaly reference sets, similarity usefulness, and follow-up
   utility need survey-, cadence-, magnitude-, and class-stratified validation.
7. **Durable live operations.** ALeRCE fetching is bounded, atomic, and now
   survey-explicit with a fail-closed qualified Rubin response contract. A
   bounded public endpoint spot check confirmed the current classifier/version
   and photometry fields, but durable watermarks, raw replay, recorded live
   fixtures, dead-letter handling, a second broker, backups,
   concurrency testing, monitoring, and incident runbooks remain future work.
8. **External reporting.** Canonical preflight exists, but there is intentionally
   no report exporter or submitter. Any future exporter must consume only a ready
   preflight result and must undergo a separate safety qualification.

## Recommended extension sequence

The next upgrades should be ordered by scientific leverage and risk reduction.

### 1. Qualify the existing system prospectively

- Freeze a campaign profile, eligibility policy, label taxonomy, review budget,
  primary metric, subgroup metrics, and withdrawal rules.
- Replay recorded TNS/SkyBoT/SIMBAD/VSX fixtures and run a zero-fail-open fault
  campaign covering timeouts, malformed results, stale data, partial publication,
  and service schema changes.
- Run heuristic, supervised baseline, anomaly reserve, and JEPA-assisted ranking
  in shadow mode on the same chronological stream.
- Report yield at fixed budget, recall/precision with uncertainty, calibration
  only where applicable, review latency, abstention rate, and failure rate.

### 2. Add durable multi-survey evidence

- Implement replayable raw broker storage with watermarks, idempotency, schema
  registry, and a second broker adapter.
- Add alert cutout/reference/difference image acquisition with content hashes and
  observation binding.
- Add survey-native forced-photometry acquisition and calibration metadata rather
  than treating supplied flux as self-authenticating.
- Build cross-survey source/alias resolution with uncertainty-aware coordinates
  and an explicit revision policy for repeated observation IDs.

### 3. Make learning outcome-driven

- Curate exact-version outcomes under a frozen taxonomy and audit label latency,
  disagreement, retractions, and selection bias.
- Add rolling-origin and site/survey holdouts, bootstrap or repeated-split
  intervals, subgroup failure analysis, and a locked untouched test set.
- Evaluate JEPA with ablations against simple temporal/statistical encoders,
  linear probes, retrieval utility, anomaly enrichment, multiple seeds, and
  compute-matched baselines.
- Promote a downstream probability only after held-out calibration and a
  preregistered prospective threshold meet declared error bounds.

### 4. Harden human and observatory operations

- Replace typed display names with authenticated principal IDs, roles, signed
  decisions, least-privilege storage, backups, and auditable deployment changes.
- Add observatory visibility, weather, facility constraints, exposure planning,
  and reasoned follow-up utility. Keep dispatch human-confirmed until separately
  qualified.
- Integrate host catalogues and population priors only after validating density,
  astrometric uncertainty, ambiguity, and survey selection effects.
- If report export is added, keep construction and transmission separate, require
  a fresh canonical preflight at both boundaries, and make submission idempotent.

## Promotion gates

Do not call IRIS operationally discovery-ready until all of the following are
true:

- zero fail-open cases in the recorded safety and publication fault suite;
- a clean install and full test matrix on every supported Python/platform target;
- locked dataset, code, config, model, token-contract, and split digests;
- prospective metrics at fixed human/follow-up budget with uncertainty and
  subgroup results;
- authenticated independent review and tested backup/recovery procedures;
- live catalogue/broker service qualification and schema monitoring;
- documented abstention, rollback, incident, and scientific-withdrawal rules; and
- independent sign-off on both scientific validity and operational safety.
